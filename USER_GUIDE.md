# SIWES Connect — User Guide

Welcome to **SIWES Connect**! This is a platform that connects Nigerian university students with organizations for their SIWES (Students Industrial Work Experience Scheme) program. This guide will walk you through everything you need to know.

---

## What is SIWES Connect?

SIWES Connect makes it easy for:

- **Students** to find and apply for SIWES placements at companies across Nigeria
- **Organizations** to post internship opportunities and manage applications
- **Coordinators** (university staff) to oversee the program and approve organizations

## For Students — Finding a Placement

### Step 1: View Available Placements

1. Log in with the student account
2. Click **"Browse Placements"** in the navigation menu
3. You will see a list of all available internship opportunities

### Step 2: Search and Filter

- Use the **search bar** to find placements by company name or job title
- Use the **state filter** to find placements in a specific location
- Use the **department filter** to find placements matching your field of study

### Step 3: View Placement Details

- Click on any placement card to see full details
- You will see: job description, requirements, location, number of slots, and application deadline

### Step 4: Apply for a Placement

- Click the **"Apply"** button on the placement you want
- Confirm your application
- You will receive a notification when your application status changes

### Step 5: Track Your Applications

1. Click **"My Applications"** in the navigation menu
2. Here you can see all the placements you have applied for
3. Each application shows its current status:
   - **SUBMITTED** — Your application has been received
   - **UNDER_REVIEW** — The organization is reviewing your application
   - **ACCEPTED** — Congratulations! Your application has been accepted
   - **REJECTED** — Unfortunately, your application was not successful
   - **WITHDRAWN** — You cancelled your application
   - **PLACEMENT_CONFIRMED** — Your placement has been confirmed

### Step 6: Manage Your Profile

1. Click **"Profile"** in the navigation menu
2. Update your personal information, department, level, and CGPA
3. Add your preferred states for placement
4. Upload your resume

### Step 7: Use the Logbook

1. Click **"Logbook"** in the navigation menu
2. Record your weekly activities during your SIWES program
3. Submit entries for your supervisor to review

## For Organizations — Managing Placements

### Step 1: Post a New Placement

1. Log in with an organization account
2. Click **"Post New Placement"** on the dashboard
3. Fill in the placement details:
   - **Title** (required) — e.g., "Software Development Intern"
   - **Description** (required) — What the intern will be doing
   - **State** (required) — Where the placement is located
   - **Total Slots** (required) — How many students you can accept
   - **Application Deadline** (required) — Last date to apply
   - **Department** — Required field of study (optional)
   - **Responsibilities** — List of duties (optional)
   - **Requirements** — What you expect from the intern (optional)
   - **Duration** — How long the internship lasts (optional)
   - **Stipend** — Whether you offer payment (optional)
4. Click **"Create Placement"**

### Step 2: Manage Your Placements

1. Click **"Manage Placements"** in the navigation menu
2. You will see all your posted placements
3. For each placement, you can:
   - Change status: **ACTIVE** (visible to students), **DRAFT** (hidden), or **CLOSED** (no longer accepting)
   - Click **"Edit"** to update the placement details

### Step 3: Review Applications

1. Click **"View Applications"** on the dashboard
2. You will see all student applications for your placements
3. For each application, you can:
   - Click **"UNDER_REVIEW"** — You are considering the application
   - Click **"ACCEPTED"** — You want to offer the student a spot
   - Click **"REJECTED"** — You are declining the application
   - Click **"PLACEMENT_CONFIRMED"** — The student has confirmed their spot

### Step 4: View Your Dashboard

The dashboard shows you a quick overview:
- Total number of placements
- Active placements
- Total applications received
- Confirmed students
- Slots filled vs. available

## For Coordinators — Overseeing the Program

### Step 1: View Your Dashboard

1. Log in with the coordinator account
2. You will see an overview of:
   - Total students on the platform
   - Total organizations
   - Pending organizations waiting for approval
   - Total placements and applications

### Step 2: Approve Organizations

1. Click **"Organizations"** in the navigation menu
2. Review organizations that have registered
3. Click **"Approve"** or **"Reject"** for each organization
4. Only approved organizations can post placements

### Step 3: View Students

1. Click **"Students"** in the navigation menu
2. See all registered students and their application status
3. Click on a student to see their full profile and application history

### Step 4: View Analytics

1. Click **"Analytics"** in the navigation menu
2. See breakdowns by:
   - Placements by state
   - Applications by status
   - Overall program statistics

### Step 5: Post Announcements

1. Click **"Announcements"** in the navigation menu
2. Create announcements for all users or specific groups (students only, organizations only)
3. Announcements appear in the notification center for all users

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: I forgot my password. What do I do?
**A:** Contact your coordinator or administrator to reset your password.

### Q: Can I apply to more than one placement?
**A:** Yes! Students can apply to multiple placements. You can track all your applications in the "My Applications" section.

### Q: How do I know if my application was accepted?
**A:** You will receive a notification when the organization reviews your application. Check the notifications bell icon or visit "My Applications" to see the status.

### Q: Can an organization see my phone number?
**A:** No. Organizations only see your name, department, and level until your application is accepted.

### Q: How do I update my profile information?
**A:** Go to the "Profile" page from the navigation menu. You can update your name, department, level, CGPA, and preferred states.

### Q: What does "PLACEMENT_CONFIRMED" mean?
**A:** This means both you and the organization have agreed to the placement. You should report to the organization on the agreed start date.

### Q: Can I withdraw my application?
**A:** Yes. Go to "My Applications" and click the "WITHDRAW" button next to the application you want to cancel.
